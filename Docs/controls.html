Driving the car

Track scene · rebindable

Defaults for the WASD layout. These are the only controls in the project that rebind — from Options or Esc ▸ Settings ▸ Controls, with a WASD layout, an Arrows layout and a reset. On a gamepad only the digital actions rebind: throttle and steering stay welded to the triggers and the left stick, because binding an analog axis to a button is offering to make the controller worse.

Arrows moves the whole control set to the right of the keyboard and drops the alternates, so the letter keys are free. Any hand edit makes the layout Custom — the two named layouts are shortcuts, not cages. Conflicting bindings are reported, not prevented: a deliberate double-bind is a legitimate thing to want, and the only difference between that and a typo is whether you meant it.

RC airplane

Create RC Plane Scene · fixed keys

The layout is Kerbal Space Program's, not a Mode 2 transmitter's. Mode 2 is what RC pilots actually fly, and it was right while the aeroplane was a flying test article — but a transmitter's value is two proportional sticks, and a key is a switch. The gamepad keeps proportional sticks; only the assignment changed.

The throttle is a ratchet. It integrates your input as a rate rather than tracking it, because a real throttle lever has no centring spring and a key and a trigger both do. Tracking directly would idle the engine the moment you let go, and hands-off flight would be impossible. The trigger is read analog, so a light pull trims and a hard one sweeps.

The six SAS modes

KeyModeWhat it holds
1Stability AssistKills rates and holds the attitude you had when you engaged it.
2ProgradePoints the nose down the velocity vector.
3RetrogradePoints it back up the velocity vector.
4TargetChases the scene target — and, on the jet, whatever the seeker has locked. Greyed out with no target.
5Wings LevelRolls level and leaves pitch to you.
6Altitude HoldHolds the altitude you engaged at, through a vertical-speed cascade.

A stick deflection past 10 % takes that axis back outright, so you never have to switch SAS off to fly.

VTOL jet

Create VTOL Jet Scene

Everything above still applies — the jet flies on the same panel model as the trainer and takes the same stick. It adds exactly one axis: the nozzles. The scene spawns you hovering at 40 m, nozzles down, SAS holding attitude and the throttle preset to the predicted hover fraction.

ActionKeyboardGamepadNotes
Nozzles aftNum 8BToward forward flight. Num 8 / Num 2 is the San Andreas Hydra's own map.
Nozzles downNum 2▼Toward the hover, and past the vertical for braking and backing up. Push forward to go faster, like everything else in a cockpit.
The nozzle lever is a second ratchet, for the same reason the throttle is one, and it sweeps its full travel in two seconds — slow enough that a transition is a manoeuvre rather than a mode flip. It deliberately survives a reset: a respawning VTOL wants the configuration it crashed in, not a surprise transition.

At the hover the control surfaces are dead — no airspeed, and no slipstream, because a jet's tail is not in a propeller wake. Control comes from puffers bleeding engine thrust to nose, tail and wingtip stations, driven by the same stick the panels take. The bleed is subtracted from the lift thrust, so full stick in the hover genuinely costs height. Puffer authority fades to zero as the nozzles swing aft, so there is no mode switch anywhere.

Weapons

jet only

Three weapons on one fire key. Press for a missile or a bomb stick, hold for the cannon. Weapons never enter the actuator vector and never touch the pilot input seam — they are not plant inputs.

ActionKeyboardGamepadNotes
Swap weaponTabXMissiles → cannon → bombs. The seeker only hunts while missiles are up.
Fire missileSpaceAPress. 8 rounds, 0.7 s between launches.
Fire cannonSpaceAHeld. Runs on heat, not ammo — hold it too long and it locks out until it cools past half.
Drop bombsSpaceAPress. A stick of six at 0.15 s intervals, released on its own clock whether or not you keep the key down.
Lock onautomaticNo scan key to hold — pointing the aeroplane is already a full-time job. The nearest valid target inside a 15° forward cone starts acquiring on its own: a twelve-segment circle fills green, then goes solid red, pulsing, with a confirmation chirp. Air targets out to 700 m, ground to 450 m.

The Hydra's hidden speed modifier is here in the open: missiles fired at air targets fly at twice the base speed. Guidance is lead pursuit at ~120°/s that drops to a crawl inside the commit range, so a hard break still defeats it — a missile that cannot be missed is a hitscan with theatrics.
