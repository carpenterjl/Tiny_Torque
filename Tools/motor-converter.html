Why this conversion exists

The simulation drives motors the way electricity does: you command a voltage, the motor's own rotation generates an opposing back-EMF, and whatever voltage is left over pushes current through the winding resistance to make torque. That needs two numbers almost no vendor prints — the torque constant Kt and the winding resistance R.

What vendors do print is Kv, or a stall torque and a no-load speed. Both roads lead to the same pair of constants, and this page walks either one.

I = (V − Kt·ωmotor) / R     τ = Kt·I · gear · η
Back-EMF rises with speed, so available current — and therefore torque — falls as the car speeds up. That is the whole shape of a DC motor's behaviour.

Route A · From Kv

The usual brushless case. Kv is revolutions per minute per volt with nothing attached.

Kt = 60 / (2π · Kv) = — N·m/A
Kt in N·m/A and Ke in V·s/rad are numerically the same constant in SI units — a happy accident of the algebra, and a useful sanity check.

Kv gives you Kt but says nothing about resistance. Measure R terminal-to-terminal with a milliohm meter, or infer it from a stall test: R ≈ V / I_stall.

Route B · From a torque/speed datasheet

Brushed motors and gearmotors are usually specified this way. All four figures must be at the same nominal voltage.

R = Vn² / (τs·ω0 + Vn·I0) = — Ω
Kt = τs·R / Vn = — N·m/A
At stall the whole rail drops across R, so τs = Kt·Vn/R. At no load the rail balances back-EMF plus the idle current's own drop, so Vn = Kt·ω0 + I0·R. Two equations, two unknowns.

One motor, several simulated ones

The sim gives every driven wheel its own motor. A real car usually has one motor feeding an axle through a differential — so entering your real figures unchanged on two wheels gives the car twice the thrust it should have.

Resistance is multiplied because several parallel current paths have to add up to the real winding; the current and inertia figures are divided because they describe one shared physical part. Torque constant, gear ratio and voltage belong to the motor itself and do not change no matter how many models represent it.
