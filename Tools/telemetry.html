What you can get out of a run

  • Step-response metrics. Choose a setpoint channel and the measurement that should follow it, and you get rise time, overshoot, settling time and steady-state error — computed the same way the game's own overlay computes them, so the numbers agree.
  • Two runs at once. Load a second CSV and both are drawn together. This is the view for comparing a sim run against a real one, or a gain change against the run before it.
  • Ground truth versus the controller's own belief. Plot veh/pos_x and veh/pos_z against whatever your firmware reports as distance. Agreement between them proves the loop closed; agreement with ground truth proves the calibration is right, and those are not the same claim.
